Enrolment forecasting

Forecasts weekly course enrolments for the next six months, with a range for how far actual numbers might fall from the forecast. It was checked against simple baselines before being trusted.

Three years of synthetic weekly data. No real platform data is used.

How it works

  • Data. 156 weeks of enrolments with steady growth, yearly seasonality, a year-end holiday dip and random noise.
  • Model. A regression on log enrolments with a linear trend, four pairs of Fourier terms for yearly seasonality and a holiday flag, fitted with NumPy least squares. Working in logs makes growth and seasonality multiplicative, which suits enrolment data.
  • Baselines. A model is only worth using if it beats simple rules. Naive repeats last week's number; seasonal naive repeats the same week last year.
  • Uncertainty. The 90% range comes from the spread of the model's residuals.
  • In the browser. The forecast is computed in Python and exported to JSON; this page draws it with Chart.js.
