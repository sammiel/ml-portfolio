Churn risk scorer

Estimates how likely a retail-bank customer is to leave in the next quarter, and shows which behaviours push that risk up or down.

Trained on 20,000 synthetic customers. No real customer data is used.

How it works

  • Data. A Python script generates 20,000 customers with tenure, products held, transactions, balance trend, complaints, app logins and whether their salary is paid into the account. The ground truth includes a non-linear tenure effect and an interaction between complaints and low engagement, so the model has something realistic to miss.
  • Model. Logistic regression on standardised features (scikit-learn), with a 75/25 stratified train/test split. A gradient-boosting model was trained as a benchmark.
  • Why the simpler model.
  • In the browser. The coefficients and scaling parameters are exported to JSON, and this page applies the same formula in JavaScript, so there's no server.
  • How it would be used. A retention team would work the top decile first, where churn runs at the average rate.
