Customer health scoring

A 0 to 100 health score for B2B learning-platform accounts, based on how likely each account is to renew. A customer success team would use it to decide which accounts to call first.

Trained on 6,000 synthetic accounts. No real customer data is used.

How it works

  • Data. A Python script generates 6,000 accounts with seat utilisation, active learners, learning hours, quarter-on-quarter utilisation change, admin logins, support tickets and whether there is a named executive sponsor, plus whether each account renewed.
  • Model. Logistic regression predicting renewal (scikit-learn), evaluated on a 25% held-out set. The health score is the predicted renewal probability multiplied by 100.
  • Why a model rather than hand-picked weights. Many health scores are weighted checklists agreed in a meeting. Fitting the weights to renewal outcomes means each input counts as much as it actually predicts renewal, and the tier table above shows whether the tiers mean something.
  • Tiers. Healthy is 70 and above, Watch is 40 to 69, and At risk is below 40. The portfolio shows 120 new accounts that were not used in training.
  • In the browser. The model is exported to JSON and scored in JavaScript, so there's no server.
